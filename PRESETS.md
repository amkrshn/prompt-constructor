# Built-in neutral presets

The repository ships generic presets only. None of the color values or font pairings are represented as a third-party brand specification.

- Business Blue — `#FFFFFF`, `#172B4D`, `#315DA8`, `#E8EFF8`
- Natural Green — `#FAFCF8`, `#203B2E`, `#287A50`, `#E7F0E8`
- Graphite / Amber — `#17202C`, `#F5F7FA`, `#F3B651`, `#344355`
- Minimal Monochrome — `#FFFFFF`, `#202124`, `#505C70`, `#EEF0F3`

Font examples: Arial; Onest; Manrope + Onest; Georgia + Arial. Availability and licensing are the responsibility of the runtime/consumer.
